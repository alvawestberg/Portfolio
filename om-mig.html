About Me

Johanna Andersson

My name is Johanna Andersson. I am 25 years old and from Borlänge. I am in my final year of the Information Systems program at Dalarna University and expect to graduate in 2027. Alongside my studies, I work part-time in IT systems administration, where I support users and troubleshoot technical issues.

I enjoy collaborating with others and exploring different ways to solve problems. I am particularly drawn to the creative side of web development because it allows me to work with both design and functionality. Outside of work and university, I enjoy exercising, travelling and spending time with family and friends.

My Interest in Web Development

I became interested in programming when I built my first web page during my studies. The project made me think about how technical decisions affect how easy a website is to use. Since then, I have wanted to learn more about website design and how the features that people use are built.

After Graduation

After graduation, I plan to work in web development and build on my skills in both front-end and back-end development. I am also exploring master's programmes in web design or web development. My long-term goal is to work as a full-stack developer, ideally in an international setting.
